Scope boundary

A business website and an application need different scopes.

Communicating an offering and capturing enquiries usually calls for a website. Business rules, data, permissions and operational integrations point to a software project. We establish that boundary before a proposal.

When we can help

Two challenges, different scopes.

The first step is understanding whether the business needs to explain its offering better or needs software to run a process.

01

Presence and communication

The current site does not explain the offering clearly, or a launch needs a page with defined content, language and action.

02

Operations and product

A process depends on spreadsheets, manual steps or disconnected tools; or an application needs to be built around its own users, rules and integrations.

If the goal is to communicate an offering and guide an action, we start with a website or landing-page scope. If the goal is to run rules and workflows with data, we assess custom software. When the solution is unclear, we first define a viable scope.

Business Websites & Landing Pages

Implementation of pages with a defined purpose, content and scope.

When it fits

To present a business or offering, support a launch or replace a website that no longer represents the business.

What we do

We build responsive pages, forms and agreed integrations, with baseline accessibility, performance and technical SEO checks. Analytics is included when scoped.

What you receive

Implemented pages, verification of agreed flows, documentation and access handover. Deployment, domain and hosting setup follow the responsibilities defined in the proposal.

Boundaries

Pages, languages, revisions and integrations are scoped. The client supplies or commissions content and translation. Ad management, conversion outcomes, search rankings and ongoing maintenance are not promised as part of this delivery.

Custom Software & Internal Tools

Applications, integrations and tools built around a workflow and clear acceptance criteria.

When it fits

When a process relies on manual work, disconnected systems or an application that still needs to be scoped. Also suitable for MVPs with defined boundaries.

What we do

What you receive
