Quality Engineering

Understand the risks. Define the next improvement.

For engineering leaders and teams that need to investigate regressions, improve automation or change a quality practice. Liberta combines assessment, implementation and handover in scoped engagements.

ASSESS · IMPLEMENT · HAND OVER

Risk, evidence and context guide the next improvement.

Method

The signal needs to support a decision.

More tests alone do not resolve a quality gap. We examine product risks, test layers, data, CI and release criteria within the agreed scope. Each conclusion states its evidence and limitations.

When we can help

Quality needs attention at different stages.

The starting point depends on the product, the team and what is already known about risk. These situations help identify the challenge; the scope follows from the context.

01

Build a foundation early

An early product team wants to define risks, ownership and useful checks before delivery volume grows.

02

Regain control

Regressions and incidents are accumulating, but it is unclear which workflows to protect first or whether current signals can be trusted.

03

Give an existing team direction

QAs and tests are already in place, but shared criteria, useful metrics or clarity about the value of quality work are missing.

04

Address a known gap

The team already knows which workflows need automation, or which specific practice needs to change, and seeks help to implement and verify the solution.

Offers

Three ways to start.

Not sure what to prioritize? Start with a diagnostic. Know which risks need automated checks? Test automation implementation. Need to put a change in practice? A scoped QE improvement.

If the cause and priorities are still unclear, the Quality Engineering Diagnostic helps define the path. If the problem is already well scoped, we can assess test automation or a specific improvement without requiring an earlier engagement.

QE-1

Quality Engineering Diagnostic

A scoped assessment to understand quality risks and decide what deserves attention first.

When it fits

When regressions, unreliable tests or unclear priorities make it difficult to decide where to invest in quality.

What we do

We review an agreed sample of code, tests, pipelines and practices. We record observations, inferences, confidence in the findings and access limitations.

What you receive

A report with findings and evidence, a risk map, priorities and recommendations, plus a session to discuss the conclusions.

Boundaries

The assessment is limited to the agreed sample and sources. Implementing the recommendations requires a separate scope. This is not product certification.

QE-2

Test Automation Implementation

Risk-based automation with actionable test results and maintainability considered from the start.

When it fits

When important workflows lack checks, or an existing suite needs new tests, stabilization or restructuring.

What we do

We define scenarios and suitable test layers, implement or improve tests, and address test data and CI integration where the environment supports it.

What you receive

Version-controlled tests and configuration, execution evidence, usage and maintenance documentation, and technical handover to the system owner.

Boundaries

Workflows, stack, environment and acceptance criteria are defined before implementation. The engagement does not include automating everything, owning all manual regression testing or providing dedicated QA staffing.

QE-3

Scoped Quality Engineering Improvement

Implementation of a specific change to a quality practice, with a defined objective, owner and completion criteria.

When it fits

When the problem calls for changes to release criteria, triage, ownership or another identified practice, beyond adding tests.

What we do

We scope the intervention, implement the change with the team and verify its use in an agreed cycle or walkthrough.

What you receive

An implemented practice or configuration, documentation, evidence of its use and an ownership handover, with remaining issues recorded.

Boundaries

Requires a sponsor and the team’s participation. It does not include ongoing fractional leadership, organization-wide transformation or unlimited support.
