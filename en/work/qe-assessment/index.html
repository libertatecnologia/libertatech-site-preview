Executive summary

The green pipeline should not be used alone as release authorization.

The synthetic corpus shows a job that can fail without blocking the aggregate pipeline and test evidence that is not sufficiently linked to the candidate. Restore traceability and visibility before adding more end-to-end tests.

02
Illustrative / Synthetic ExampleF04 / CIHigh confidence in supplied corpus

A green pipeline is not enough to decide.

Observation
In the synthetic sample, the E2E job fails while the overall pipeline remains green.
Evidence
SYN-02 allows the job failure to leave the pipeline unblocked. SYN-03, run J04, illustrates that outcome.
Inference
The aggregate status is insufficient as a release criterion. The failure still needs triage to distinguish product, environment and test issues.
Recommendation
Separate attempts, persistent failures and quarantined tests. Define critical checks and test the gate with a controlled failure.

Limitation. High within the supplied synthetic corpus. Does not represent certainty about a real system.

07
30 / 60 / 90 view

An illustrative sequence, not a delivery promise

  1. 0–30

    Make the signal trustworthy

    Separate attempts and final state, identify the candidate, isolate fixtures and define release ownership.

    Gate

    Demonstrate a controlled gate failure.

  2. 31–60

    Protect critical booking rules

    Map rule → risk → test or control; add agreed conflict, adjacency and concurrency coverage.

    Gate

    Verify persistent state and candidate evidence.

  3. 61–90

    Sustain the practice

    Review test layers, document maintenance and revisit risk exceptions.

    Gate

    The team can maintain the signal without permanent consulting support.

08
Methodology and limitations

The sample teaches a method; it does not simulate a client result

Sources. SYN-01 to SYN-08 are invented and reproduced in the public Markdown sample. No external evidence exists behind them.

Confidence. High means a direct relationship to the supplied synthetic corpus; medium means partial reach or confirmation is required.

Boundaries. No full codebase, database queries, security, load, accessibility, production telemetry, interviews or real executions were assessed.
