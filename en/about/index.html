Operating model

Every project has a scope and named owners.

Liberta operates with a lean structure and direct technical leadership. The proposal identifies who delivers, who reviews and who receives the work. Any additional collaboration depends on the project’s needs and confirmed availability.

  1. Evidence and context before recommendations.
  2. Scope and responsibility before commitment.
  3. Human review of technical decisions, including work supported by AI.
  4. Verification and handover as part of delivery.
